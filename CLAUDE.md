# Resume Project Guidelines

## Syncing Rules

Any change made to a specialized resume (`Ryan Peach - Cloud Infrastructure.tex`, `Ryan Peach - Data Engineering.tex`, etc.) must also be reflected in `Ryan Peach - Complete Resume.tex`. The Complete Resume must always be a superset of all specialized resumes.

## Review Process

Every update must be proposed line by line and reviewed by the author before being written to any file. There are no automatic updates — always show the proposed change and wait for explicit approval.
